# User Guide

## 1. Create a user ID

Open the application, choose **Create ID**, and enter:

- full name;
- unique username;
- unique email address;
- password with at least eight characters.

Every invited team member must register first.

## 2. Choose an organization option

After registration, organization creation is optional. The onboarding screen offers three choices:

- **Create an organization** — the creator becomes CEO and receives immediate workspace access. Default `general` and `project-updates` channels are created.
- **Join an organization** — accept an invitation sent to the registered username or email, then wait for CEO/admin approval.
- **Do this later** — keep the account in account-only mode. The dashboard, projects, tasks, channels, reports, team members, and analytics remain locked until an active organization membership exists.

## 3. Invite a team member

Open **Admin dashboard** and enter the exact registered username or email address. Choose the proposed role allowed by your own role.

The invited user does not gain access yet.

## 4. Accept and approve joining

The invited user signs in and accepts the invitation. Its status changes to `awaiting_approval`.

A CEO or admin then opens **Admin dashboard** and selects **Approve access**. Only after this approval does the organization become visible to the invited user.

## 5. Manage team access

The Admin dashboard shows all memberships. Depending on permissions, managers can:

- change role;
- suspend or reactivate a member;
- remove a member;
- review invitation history;
- approve or reject accepted invitations.

CEO membership is protected. Only the CEO can grant or modify admin access.

## 6. Use channels

Choose **Channels**, select a channel, and post messages. CEO, admin, and moderator roles can create additional channels.

## 7. Manage projects

CEO, admin, and moderator roles can create a project. Active organization members can be assigned to tasks.

Available project areas include:

- dashboard and Kanban overview;
- work breakdown and AI plan generation;
- meeting-note proposals;
- risk scanning and decision history;
- change control;
- factual reports and export.

AI-generated tasks, meeting-note proposals, risks, and changes retain human review controls.

## 8. View the people directory

Open **People** to see every organization member. Each card shows avatar, role, department, membership state, live presence, workspace status, status note, email, and last active time. Use search, department, and presence filters to narrow the directory.

## 9. Manage your profile and workspace status

Open **Profile** to update your full name and optional HTTPS avatar. Choose a workspace status such as Available, Busy, On Leave, Remote, In a Meeting, Focus Time, or Travelling. A custom emoji and label can also be created.

The workspace-status badge appears beside your name in channels, the People directory, member administration, task ownership, Kanban cards, and the sidebar. A separate optional status note can add context such as “Available after 3 PM.”

## 10. Configure appearance and presence

Open **Settings** and choose:

- **Light** — always use the light interface;
- **Dark** — always use the dark interface;

The same page controls the live presence dot: Automatic, Always online, Away, Do not disturb, or Appear offline. Automatic presence uses browser heartbeat activity.

## 11. Use notifications

Open **Notifications** to review organization invitations, access approvals/rejections, membership updates, and channel @mentions. Unread items show a navigation badge. Each notification can be marked read individually, or all unread items can be cleared together.

Notification categories can be enabled or disabled from **Settings**.

## 12. Review account activity

Open **Account activity** to see a private history of account creation, sign-ins, profile changes, settings changes, status updates, organization creation, invitation actions, and membership events.

## 13. Switch or create organizations

Use the organization selector in the sidebar. On mobile, use the organization selector in the top bar. Select **New organization** or the plus button to create another workspace; Orbit switches to it immediately.

## 14. Assign departments and manage invitations

When sending an invitation, choose the proposed role and department. After approval, both values are applied to the membership. CEO/admin can later update the role, department, or active/suspended membership state. Managers can cancel invitations allowed by their role hierarchy while they remain open.
